AssetsHost — Demo
=================

This is a live demo of AssetsHost, a self-hosted asset CDN.

Everything here is for show:
  - the files below are bundled samples
  - renaming, listing, and deleting look real but never persist
  - uploads are faked in your browser and vanish on reload

Nothing you do here touches a real database or storage bucket.
